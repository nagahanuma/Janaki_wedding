## ✨ Features
- **Traditional Temple Door Welcome Gate:** Interactive sliding door overlay with custom couple monogram (`S ♥ J`) and auspicious temple bells.
- **Multilingual Support (EN | தமிழ் | తెలుగు):**
  - Instant zero-reload language switching.
  - 3-step automatic detection: URL query param (`?lang=ta` / `?lang=te`) → Browser language → IP Geolocation (Tamil Nadu / Andhra Pradesh / Telangana).
- **Cute 3D Couple Hero Illustration:** Exact 3D cartoon couple art from the official physical invitation, seated in front of the sacred Homa Kunda and brass Kalashas.
- **Live Countdown Timer:** Live ticking countdown to the auspicious Muhurtham (**Monday, November 16, 2026 at 5:00 AM IST**).
- **Modern Jewel-Tone Event Cards:**
  1. **Reception & Dinner:** Sunday, November 15, 2026 (7:00 PM onwards)
  2. **Upanayanam Ceremony:** Monday, November 16, 2026 (4:00 AM – 5:00 AM)
  3. **Wedding Muhurtham:** Monday, November 16, 2026 (5:00 AM – 6:00 AM) with Kalyana Virundhu (Wedding Feast)
  - One-click **Google Maps navigation** and downloadable **`.ics` calendar reminders**.
- **One-Tap WhatsApp RSVP:** Guests can select their attendance and confirm directly with a pre-filled WhatsApp message.
- **Interactive Audio & Falling Flower Petals:** HTML5 marigold and jasmine flower petals canvas rain with floating ambient music toggle.
- **Mobile-First Luxury Aesthetic:** Sabyasachi Royal Carmine, Imperial Temple Emerald, Champagne Gold, and Pearl Silk Cream palette.

## 🚀 How to Enable GitHub Pages
1. Go to this repository on GitHub: **[https://github.com/nagahanuma/Janaki_wedding](https://github.com/nagahanuma/Janaki_wedding)**
2. Click on **Settings** (top menu).
3. On the left sidebar, click **Pages**.
4. Under **Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and folder `/ (root)`.
5. Click **Save**.
6. Within 1–2 minutes, your wedding invitation website will be live at:
   `https://nagahanuma.github.io/Janaki_wedding/`
